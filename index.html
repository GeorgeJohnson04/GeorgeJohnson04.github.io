About

A bit about me

I'm an economist and analyst based in Houston. I finished my B.S. in Economics & Public Policy at the University of Houston, and I'm now doing a master's in Applied Economics at Johns Hopkins.

The last couple of years took me through energy trading at Enbridge, public finance at Harris County, market research at the Asia Society, and a year of econometrics research at UH. I'm usually the person who gets handed the messy spreadsheet and turns it into something useful — a model, a macro, or a clean report.

I work mostly in Python, R, STATA, SQL, and VBA, and I like careful work that other people can actually rely on.

  • Audited 150+ daily commodity trades and reconciled mark-to-market P&L at Enbridge.
  • Built VBA & Python automation that cut manual processing by up to 95%.
  • Ran econometric analysis on 1M+ data points in R, STATA & Python.
